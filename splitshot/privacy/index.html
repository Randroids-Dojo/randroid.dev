SplitShot privacy policy

Effective October 11, 2026

SplitShot is published by Randroid Apps, a brand of Randroid LLC. Privacy contact: support@randroid.dev.

Images and annotations stay on your device

SplitShot accesses only the image you choose with the Android Photo Picker or share from another app. It makes a private temporary copy to display and annotate that image and to export at the original resolution. Your notes and drawing coordinates are processed in memory on your device. SplitShot does not upload images, annotations, or device information to us or to a server.

Saving and sharing

Save writes a new PNG to Pictures/SplitShot in your photo library. Your original image is not overwritten. Share prepares a temporary PNG and opens the Android share sheet. The receiving app you choose is granted access to that PNG. What that app does with the image is governed by its own privacy practices. Any backup or synchronization of images saved in your photo library is controlled by your photo-library settings and services.

Temporary files and deletion

The app attempts to delete its private source copy when the editing session ends or when you successfully open a replacement image. When an image is opened, leftover source and share files older than 24 hours are removed. Preparing another share also removes share files older than one hour. These cleanups run when those actions occur; they are not scheduled background deletion. Android may clear cached files sooner. Clear SplitShot storage or uninstall it to remove its private files immediately. Saved gallery images and copies held by other apps remain until you delete them there. Editable annotations are not retained as projects after the editor is closed.

Permissions, accounts, and tracking

SplitShot does not request internet, camera, microphone, location, contacts, or broad photo-library access. It has no accounts, ads, analytics, advertising identifiers, or remote crash-reporting services. Private app data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer. Google Play handles your app purchase under its own privacy and payment policies; SplitShot does not ask for your payment details.

Support and this website

If you email support@randroid.dev, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond. Send only information needed for your request. We retain support correspondence as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations; you can contact us to request deletion. Visiting the online policy through a browser may provide standard connection information, such as your IP address, to our website hosting provider to serve the page. This does not upload your images from SplitShot.

Changes and questions

We will update this policy and its effective date when our practices change. Questions about privacy or deletion can be sent to support@randroid.dev. Online policy: https://randroid.dev/splitshot/privacy/.
