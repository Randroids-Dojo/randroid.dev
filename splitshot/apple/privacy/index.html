SplitShot for Apple — privacy policy

Effective October 11, 2026. SplitShot is published by Randroid Apps, a brand of Randroid LLC. Contact: support@randroid.dev.

Images and annotations stay on your device

SplitShot reads only the image you choose in Photos or Files, open on your Mac, or share to its iPhone/iPad extension. It decodes the selected image and keeps notes and drawing coordinates in memory on your device. It does not upload images, annotations, or device information to us or to a server. It has no accounts, ads, analytics, advertising identifiers, or remote crash-reporting service.

Saving and sharing

On iPhone and iPad, Save asks for permission to add a new PNG to Photos. This does not grant access to browse your photo library. If you decline that permission, you can save through Files instead. On Mac, Save uses the system file dialog. You choose where to put the PNG. The app never modifies the imported image.

Share prepares a temporary PNG and opens the system sharing controls. The app or recipient you choose receives that PNG. Its privacy practices govern what happens next. Any backup or synchronization of saved images is controlled by your Photos, Files, iCloud, or other storage settings and services.

Temporary files and deletion

Editable notes are not stored as projects. Closing the editor discards that session. Sharing creates a PNG in the app's private temporary directory. Temporary exported PNGs older than 24 hours are removed the next time the app launches. The operating system may clear temporary files sooner. Uninstalling the app removes its private files. Saved images and copies shared to other apps remain until you delete them there.

Support

If you email support@randroid.dev, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond. Send only what is needed for your request. We retain correspondence as needed to resolve requests and meet applicable obligations; contact us to request deletion. Visiting our online policy through a browser may provide standard connection information to our website hosting provider. It does not upload your images from SplitShot.

Changes and questions

We will update this policy and its effective date when our practices change. Questions about privacy or deletion can be sent to support@randroid.dev.
