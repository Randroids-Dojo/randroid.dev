SplitShot for Apple

Keep the original. Put your thoughts beside it.

SplitShot is a native image annotation app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Mark a passage in an article or a line of code, then add a connected note. Export one PNG with the untouched original on the left and your annotations on the right.

Get help

Email support@randroid.dev. Include your device, operating system, app version, and what you expected to happen. Share an example image only if you are comfortable sending its contents.

Open, annotate, export

On iPhone and iPad, open an image from Photos or Files, or choose SplitShot from another app’s image share sheet. On Mac, open or drop an image into the editor.

Tap or click to add a connected note. Drag for a freehand mark, or hold then drag to highlight text. There is no drawing mode to switch. Use Done to finish typing, and undo or redo to adjust your work.

Use two fingers to pan and zoom on iPhone and iPad. On Mac, scroll or pinch with a trackpad; Option-drag also pans. Fit brings the whole image back into view.

Save creates a PNG exactly twice the source image’s width, at its original height. The original image is never overwritten. On iPhone and iPad, Save adds a photo; if permission is declined, choose a file destination instead. On Mac, choose a destination in the save dialog. Share opens the system sharing controls.

Before closing

Editable sessions are temporary. Save or share your PNG before closing or opening another image. PNGs preserve the finished result; they do not reopen as editable projects.

The main editor accepts images up to 16 million pixels. The iPhone/iPad share extension accepts up to 4 million pixels; use the main app for larger images. Images that exceed the limit are rejected with an explanation rather than resized.

Your images stay on your device

SplitShot has no accounts, ads, analytics, or image uploads. Read the Apple app privacy policy for details about saving, sharing, and temporary files.
